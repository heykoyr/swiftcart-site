Getting on SwiftCart

Simple by design.
Invited by your operator.

Vendors are invited by a delivery company operating in their area. That is how we make sure every shop on the marketplace can actually be fulfilled.

01

Receive an invite

A delivery company sends an invitation to your phone number after confirming it can cover your shop.

02

Claim your shop

Sign in to the SwiftCart Vendor app with that number, complete your details and set your opening hours.

03

Get ready to sell

Add your logo, products, prices and stock. Once approved, customers can discover your shop.

Clear economics

Your item price
is your money.

No SwiftCart vendor fee

SwiftCart charges vendors no subscription and takes no commission from the subtotal of items they sell. You are credited for each delivered order.

One delivery company at a time

Your company handles collection, delivery and settlement. It may agree separate commercial terms with you, but that is not a SwiftCart platform fee.
