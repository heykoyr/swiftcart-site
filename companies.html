What you get

A national storefront.
Your operation underneath.

SwiftCart is the consumer brand customers see. Your rider and company-admin applications carry your company identity, while the delivery operation stays entirely in your control.

Customers without an app launch

Your stores appear in the SwiftCart marketplace alongside other nearby stores. You do not have to build a consumer app or persuade users to download one.

Your rider and admin brand

Set your logo, colours and support contacts. Your riders and operations team work in applications that feel like your system—because they are.

The full operations suite

Dispatch, live tracking, payouts, disputes, refunds, inventory, promotions, fraud flags, audit logs and customer marketing from one platform.

Company onboarding

Configured for your business.
Ready in under an hour.

Once your payment key is ready, company setup is deliberate and fast. The system will not take customer payments until the licensed operator is payment-ready.

The economics are intentionally clear

You keep the commission.
SwiftCart never touches order money.

Your payment account, your economics

Customer payments go directly into your licensed Paystack account. You set delivery rates, commission and withdrawal thresholds, then settle vendors and riders from your own balance.

A fixed software subscription

SwiftCart earns from a monthly or yearly company subscription agreed off-app. There is no revenue share, per-order fee or percentage of your commission.
